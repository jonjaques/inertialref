# The timeline's shares, and the declared bytes

Every pass the clouds add, as its share of the candidate's frames from
`ir.passes()`, in ms, for every quiet row of campaign 1's `decisions` batch
(`8a01d311`) and campaign 2's `targets` batch (`bbea04da`). A share is
`passes.labels['<label>'].ms` in the row documents, which stay on the Mac mini
under `.data/clouds/`; the campaigns' summaries print the largest four and the
bakes' sum, and this file prints each. A dash is a pass the row's frame does
not run. A share is not a cost: a row's cost is `ir.gpu()` against clouds
off, in the summaries.
