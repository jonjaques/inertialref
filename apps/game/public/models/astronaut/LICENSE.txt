NASA generally permits its polygon data and texture maps in computer graphical
simulations. Credit is given to NASA and the named source. NASA names, emblems,

contains no NASA logos, agency patches, name labels, or identifiable human face.
NASA does not endorse InertialRef.

The model is adapted by the InertialRef project: the backpack is removed; the
surface uses neutral ivory fabric and an opaque gold visor; the mesh is softened,
weighted to a 39-bone humanoid armature, and supplied with original idle, walk,
run, crouch, crouch-walk, left/right strafe, jump, fall and fly animations.
